# Cushion Support: launch ad pack (hemorrhoids, women 40+ first)

These are native, first-person scripts for **real creators or customers** to record or post, plus brand-voice advertorials. The angle is the Venalys mechanism (the tissue cushions under the surface, which creams don't reach), aimed at **women**, where searches are growing (+300% to +2,900%).

**Rules:**
- **No cure claims:** don't say "gone forever" or "stay free".
- **No made-up doctors or credentials.**
- **Testimonials must be real** and represent typical results.
- Every claim gets an asterisk and the FDA disclaimer.

---

## Day-1 test: 3 angles × 4 hooks

### Angle A: "the cream drawer" (tried everything)
1. "I counted. Eleven tubes of hemorrhoid cream in my bathroom drawer. Every one worked for about four hours."
2. "Nobody tells you creams only touch the surface. The swelling starts underneath."
3. "I spent more on hemorrhoid cream last year than on my car insurance. Here's what I tried instead."
4. "If your cream works until lunch and then it's back, read this."

**Body (60–90 sec story):** first the tried list (creams, wipes, sitz baths, fiber, the donut cushion). Then the moment of realizing they all work on the surface. Then the mechanism: the cushions of tissue and the veins under them lose support with age and pregnancy. Then the routine: two capsules a day, at least 90 days. Then an honest result in her own words, like "for me, sitting through my granddaughter's recital wasn't a negotiation anymore."* Close on the 90-day guarantee and discreet shipping.

### Angle B: "it started after my kids" (postpartum, women 35–55)
1. "My youngest is 19. My hemorrhoids are 19."
2. "Pregnancy gave me two kids and one problem I never told anyone about."
3. "The OB said it would go away after the baby. That was 2009."
4. "If yours started after pregnancy and never fully stopped, this is for you."

**Body:** pregnancy stretches and weakens the support tissue, and it often never fully recovers. That's why creams keep "working" and the flare-ups keep coming back. Then the daily routine, a real result, and the guarantee.

### Angle C: "the life it takes" (Level 3–4 pain, from Reddit language)
1. "I chose the aisle seat at every wedding for six years so nobody would see me shift."
2. "I stopped saying yes to road trips and blamed my back."
3. "My husband thought I was avoiding him. I was avoiding sitting down."
4. "I planned my whole day around where I could sit, and nobody knew why."

**Body:** the hiding, the excuses, missing out. Then "I finally looked up why the cream never lasted." Then the mechanism, then the routine. The result is stated as a feeling, not a cure: "I don't plan around it anymore."* Then the guarantee.

## Pages and structure
- **Launch from 1–3 pages:** your brand page, plus real creator or customer pages through partnership (whitelisted) ads. Use real people, not invented personas.
