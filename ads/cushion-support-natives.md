# Cushion Support: native ads from Venalys's top 3 angles

Venalys's top angles (finish line, expert confession, flare-ups) mostly run as **statics or expert posts**. The hooks already work in their statics ("Third flare this year?", "Scam exposed", "Doctors issue a warning?", "F*ck you, hemorrhoids", "Final notice"). This pack rewrites each angle as a **native first-person post** that a real customer or creator records or posts from their own page (as a whitelisted partnership ad).

**Rules (same as the launch pack):**
- **Real people only**: no invented doctors, nurses or credentials. "Expert" natives are a customer retelling what *their own* doctor said, or a real licensed creator speaking in their own name.
- **No cure claims**: no "gone forever" or "stay free". Results are feelings and routines, in the person's own words, and must be typical.
- Fill in `[brackets]` with the real person's details. Asterisk the claims and add the FDA disclaimer.

**Native format:** a lo-fi phone photo (bathroom counter, car, kitchen table; never a studio shot), text-heavy first person, the product shown only in the second half, and a plain headline in the link card.
