## Claims

Supplement copy follows FDA structure/function rules. No claim to diagnose, treat, cure or prevent a disease.

**OK to use (already on the PDP):**
- Supports digestive comfort
- Helps with bloating after meals
- Fewer urgent bathroom trips (as a customer-reported outcome)
- Supports the gut's natural cleaning cycle
- 90-day money-back guarantee

**Do not use on ads:**
- Comparisons to Rifaximin or any prescription drug
- "Antimicrobial", "clears overgrowth", "kills bacteria/parasites" (read as SIBO or disease claims)
- Naming a disease (SIBO, IBS, IBD)
- Before/after body shots implying a medical result

**VERIFY before use:** "Trusted by 100,000+ customers", "10,000+ scientific studies", "Backed by clinical research".
