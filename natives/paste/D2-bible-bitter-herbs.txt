Pigs carry bugs.

Pork can also carry tapeworm, which can grow several feet long in a human gut and send its eggs up to the brain. And right along with them come the bacteria that make people sick for days.

Three thousand years before anyone had a microscope, people in the desert were told to keep away from the one animal most likely to put something living inside them.
