# Store policies

Written Oct 15, 2026 from what the product page promises (90-day money-back guarantee, free shipping, US only). Shopify's API connection here can't save policies, so paste them in by hand.

## How to paste them in (about 5 minutes)

1. Shopify admin → **Settings → Policies**.
2. Open a policy, click the **`<>`** (Show HTML) button in the editor toolbar, select everything in the box, and paste the matching file's contents. Click `<>` again to check it looks right, then **Save**.

| Shopify policy | File |
|---|---|
| Refund policy | `refund-policy.html` |
| Shipping policy | `shipping-policy.html` |
| Terms of service | `terms-of-service.html` |
| Privacy policy (replace the current one) | `privacy-policy.html` |
| Contact information | `contact-information.html` |
| Subscription policy (only appears once a subscription plan exists) | `subscription-policy.html` |

The footer already links policies automatically, and checkout shows them too.

## Promises these policies make (keep them true)

- Refunds: full refund within 90 days of delivery, no need to send bottles back, reply within 2 business days.
- Damaged or wrong items: replacement or refund if reported within 30 days.
- Shipping: free, US only, ships in 1 to 2 business days, most orders arrive in 3 to 5 business days.
- Lost packages: replacement or refund if not delivered 10 business days after shipping.
- Buy 3 Get 2 Free orders: packed first and sent expedited at no extra cost (the "Top Priority Order" gift).
- Subscriptions: cancel any time, at least 2 days before the next charge; price changes emailed in advance.

Change any of these in the file before pasting if they don't match how you actually operate.
