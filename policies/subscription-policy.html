Subscribe & Save

When you choose Subscribe & Save, we'll send you a new order of Veltia automatically on the schedule you picked, and charge your payment method for each shipment at the subscription price. The price, how often it ships and the first charge are shown on the product page and at checkout before you pay.

Charges

  • Your first order is charged when you check out.
  • Each following order is charged automatically on its scheduled date, to the payment method you used at checkout, until you cancel.
  • We'll email you a confirmation each time an order is charged and shipped.
  • If we ever change the price of your subscription, we'll email you before the change takes effect, so you can cancel if you'd like.

Cancel any time

There's no minimum number of orders and no cancellation fee. You can cancel, pause, skip a shipment or change how often it ships:

  • from your account on our website, using the link in your order confirmation email; or
  • by emailing shopveltialabs@gmail.com.

To stop your next order, cancel at least 2 days before its charge date. If you're charged after cancelling, email us and we'll refund that charge in full.

Refunds

Every subscription shipment is covered by our 90-day money-back guarantee. See our Refund Policy for details.

Payment problems

If a payment fails, we'll email you and try again over the next few days. If we can't charge your payment method, your subscription will be paused until you update it.
