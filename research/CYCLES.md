# Sweep cycles (every 2 hours)

Each cycle searches 4 desire keywords + 6 general keywords (two sort orders each, US / English only), then checks the full ad history of the shortlist and the top 10 new health stores.

**STRONG** = first ad within 28 days, posted in the last 3 days, ≤10% cut, and 150+ total ads (or 25+ captured on 3+ pages). **PASS** = fresh, posting, clean. **WATCH** = posting but fails a rule.
