## 3. Who Biora's natives speak to

- **Every one is about urgency and diarrhea** (IBS-D, Imodium, bathroom mapping).
- **Every narrator is a woman aged about 44–56**, or a "doctor".
- **Every voice is a persona:** an invented doctor, nurse or "Susan". None uses a real customer, the founder, a partner or a caregiver.
- **The cause is always the gut's "cleaning wave"** (ginger), sometimes with "biofilm". Constipation, methane, slow transit and the mental toll never appear.

## 4. What Biora is not covering

Ranked by how close each one stays to what's already working (the urgency lead).

1. **Real customers instead of personas.** We now have 4 real customers with urgency stories (Jessica, Rachel, Michelle, Amanda). A native built on a real story answers "success stories are fake ads". It also keeps the ad account safer than invented doctors, and it's the same angle.
2. **Side effects and fear of starting.** It's the #1 complaint in the category and Biora never mentions it. Veltia can answer it inside an urgency native: start with a few drops, build up slowly, a bitter but honest taste, the 90-day guarantee. Same angle, one more objection handled.
3. **The mental toll.** Anxiety (130 docs), depression (106) and loneliness (58) are huge in the research and barely touched. "Terrified of being somewhere without a bathroom" is already in Amanda's real review. It fits the urgency lead; just keep it to feelings, with no mental-health claims.
4. **Shame and appearance.** "Look pregnant" and clothes are the most visual pain in the research, and Biora only mentions them as a result. It can sit inside urgency stories as "the bloating that comes with it" (Jessica's review already does this).
5. **A partner's point of view.** A spouse watching it happen (research Post 39). It's a new voice on the same pain, and nobody is running it.
6. **Constipation, methane and slow transit.** Constipation is about 3× urgency in the research (88 vs. 33 docs), and Biora has zero coverage. Our top mechanism for garlic, the "Methane Brake", lives here. It's the biggest open pool, but it's a new angle, so it's a later test once the urgency natives are proven.
7. **Younger women (25–40).** Biora's narrators are 44–56. The research skews 25–45. Same pain, younger story (work, dating, a wedding).

## 5. Recommendation

Keep the urgency lead Biora has proven. Win on what they don't do:
- real voices;
- handling side-effect fear;
- the emotional and mental toll;
- appearance as a secondary pain.

Hold constipation and methane as the first new-angle test once the D-series is running.
