# Combined product research: Reddit demand × Meta supply × Google × CJ

Two methods, one pipeline:

| Step | Source | Answers | Method |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Demand | Reddit (~68,000 posts) | Is the pain deep, unsolved, shared, and solvable by a product? | Pain Finder: pain phrases, the 4 depth levels, desires, "tried everything", "cause unknown", product vs prescription mentions |
| 2. Growth | Google Trends + autocomplete | Is search interest rising? How do people search for it? | Year-on-year change, 2-year slope, rising and breakout queries |
| 3. Supply | Meta Ads Library | Is a fresh operator scaling ads on it right now? | Your rabbit-hole: exact-phrase search, then the 28-day / 1–3-day cadence / no-old-ads rules |
| 4. Sourcing | CJ Dropshipping | Can I get it, at what price, and in which formats? | `cj --formats`: same ingredient as powder, gummy, spray, patch… (results arriving from the CJ session) |
| 5. Score | Your 7-row playbook, plus Reddit numbers | Test it, or skip? | Only test at 28+ / 35 |

**The rule:** a winner needs **deep Reddit pain** *and* **rising Google interest** *and* **a fresh store scaling on Meta** (or a clear gap where nobody is advertising yet), *and* **a sourceable format**.

## The bridge: Reddit's level 3–4 language as new Meta search phrases

Native ads that work usually lift their hooks straight from this kind of language. None of these phrases are in your already-searched lists, and apostrophes are removed because exact-phrase search breaks on them.

**Identity loss (L4):** "I used to be able to", "shell of myself", "the old me", "I lost myself", "I dont recognize myself", "I miss being able to", "who I used to be", "not the same person"
**Failing a role:** "feel like a burden", "feel like a bad mom", "the mom I want to be", "the dad I want to be", "keep up with my kids", "play with my grandkids"
**Comparison and missing out:** "everyone else gets to", "people my age", "I lost my 20s", "missing out on", "on the sidelines"
**Shame and hiding:** "too embarrassed to", "nobody knows", "make excuses", "being judged", "hide my arms", "even with good hygiene", "shower twice a day"
**Future dread:** "for the rest of my life", "is this my life now", "will I ever be normal"
**Cause unknown:** "bloodwork came back normal", "no one believes me", "doctors dont know", "brushed me off", "misdiagnosed for"
**Desire:** "feel normal again", "wear shorts again", "go to the beach", "date again", "travel without worrying"
**Body odor:** "body odor", "I smell even after", "internal deodorant", "chlorophyll", "TMAU", "smell like onions"

Your own tip still applies: phrases with fewer than about 300 results find the fresh stores.

## Ranked plan

1. **Keep Biora (IBS/SIBO) as the main product.** Reddit confirms it is the biggest and deepest gut pain pool, and SIBO searches are rising. Use the Reddit L3–4 language in the persona ads: "I can't make plans or travel without worrying about my stomach", "I miss being able to go out and have drinks with my friends", "I just want to feel like me again".
2. **Venalys (hemorrhoids) as the next launch: still a go, with eyes open.** Its edge is messaging, not unmet demand: Reddit and Google are both flat. Lean into the **female** angle, where searches are growing (+300% to +2,900%), and soften the "stay free" cure claim.
3. **Run Meta on body odor next.** It is the strongest unclaimed demand signal across all three Reddit sets, and it scores 31/35 on your playbook. If the rabbit-hole finds no fresh operator, the space is open. If it finds one inside the 28-day window, that operator is your way in.
4. **Neuropathy is the sleeper.** Product mentions beat prescriptions, the L4 pain is real, Nervalley is still scaling, and "how I cured my neuropathy" is up 800%.
5. **Lydra and lymphatic: ride the wave only with standout messaging.** It's the fastest-growing search, but ads built the demand and the space is crowded. The deeper demand underneath is **bloating** (×1.39, "I look pregnant"), which also feeds Biora.
6. **Skip or deprioritize:** endometriosis, anastrozole, lipedema, and pelvic floor (all doctor-led). Blood sugar is Ozempic-driven and risky to claim against.
