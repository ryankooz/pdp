## Takeaways
- Expert-persona and mechanism ads make up 6 of the top 10. The authority voice plus "collagen, not veins" is the core of the winning formula.
- The two brand/offer ads are the most widely spread creatives (3–4 pages each). They're the scaled closers.
- The newest tests (Oct 18–22) are shock-opener customer stories (trucker, raw beef) and a **postpartum** page. Venalys is pushing into the women/postpartum angle, which matches the rising search data.
- Format: Meta's filter counts ~71 image ads vs ~31 video ads. **Image + long copy dominates**, and the top creatives run in both formats.
- Compliance reminder: the surgeon/nurse personas and "stay gone" claims are invented authority and cure claims. Copy the structure, not the fake credentials.
